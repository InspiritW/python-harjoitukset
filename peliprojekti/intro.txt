Welcome to Codebreaker!

You are a codebreaker who has found a hidden tower of riddles.
Every riddle you solve earns you coins.
Spend your coins in the shop on potions, and see how far you can get.
Don't throw anything away: explore the tower, find old scraps and recycle them for coins.

Your goal is to crack the final code and open the tower door.
There are three ways to do it: be a riddle master, a clever merchant, or a recycler.
