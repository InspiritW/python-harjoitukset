How to play:
- Play opens the next riddle that you have not solved yet.
- During a riddle you can type 'hint' if you want help.
- Solving a riddle without a hint earns 3 coins. With a hint you earn 1 coin.
- Shop sells potions for coins.
- Inventory shows the potions you own.
- Quit saves your game, so you can continue later.
